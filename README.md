My first still life is a palm tree with coconuts. My second still life is a person shooting a basketball at a hoop. I got the idea for the palm tree because the first ASCII art I remember seeing was one of a palm tree, so I thought that it would would be cool to recreate it, even though the palm tree I made was a lot smaller. I got the idea for the basketball player and hoop because I used to play basketball, and the palm tree reminded me of beach volleyball, which reminded me of sports.

I started by sketching out both still lifes on the terminal grid paper. Here is an image of what I sketched:

![Still life sketches](still_life_sketches.png)

Then, after knowing what characters to put in which spots, I filled in all the correct spots in char[][] getPalmTree and char[][] getBasketball. After, I filled in and coded void main() and void scrollArt().

I got the ASCII art for the palm tree from Dani at https://asciiart.cc/view/10826 (citation points: 3). I created the ASCII art for the person shooting the basketball and the basketball hoop by myself. For the person, I used this image: https://www.shutterstock.com/image-vector/basketball-player-shooting-ball-side-view-1155740173?dd_referrer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2F. I created the basketball hoop myself without a specific reference (citation points: 1).
